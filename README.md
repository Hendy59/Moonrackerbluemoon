# Moonrackers Digital Board Game

A web-based digital adaptation of the Moonrackers board game by IV Studios.

## About

Moonrackers is a deck-building game where players compete as space companies to fulfill contracts, gather resources, and earn prestige by completing missions in the solar system.

## Features

- **Contract Fulfillment System**: Complete various space missions by gathering required resources
- **Resource Management**: Collect and manage Energy, Parts, Science, and Exotic Matter
- **Market System**: Purchase resources and hire crew members
- **Deck Building**: Play cards from your hand to gather resources and advance your strategy
- **Turn-based Gameplay**: Progress through phases and turns to build your space empire
- **Victory Condition**: First player to reach 20 prestige points wins!

## How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Start with 10 credits and an empty resource pool
3. Use the Action Phase to:
   - Play cards from your hand to gather resources
   - Purchase items from the market
   - Complete contracts by spending resources
4. Progress through phases using the "Next Phase" button
5. Earn prestige points by completing contracts
6. Reach 20 prestige points to win!

## Game Components

### Resources
- 🔋 **Energy**: Power for your operations
- ⚙️ **Parts**: Mechanical components
- 🧪 **Science**: Research and development
- 💎 **Exotic Matter**: Rare materials for advanced missions

### Contracts
Complete various missions like:
- Lunar Mining Operations
- Mars Research Stations
- Asteroid Belt Surveys
- Deep Space Probes

Each contract requires specific resources and rewards you with prestige and credits.

### Market
Purchase resources and hire crew members to boost your capabilities.
